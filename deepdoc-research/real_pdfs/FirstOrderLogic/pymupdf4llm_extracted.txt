# First-order Logic with Being a Thesis Modal Operator 

### Marcin Łyczak 

##### **Abstract** 

We introduce syntactic modal operator ✷ **_⊢_** for _being a thesis_ into firstorder logic. This logic is a modern realization of R. Carnap’s old ideas on modality, as logical necessity (J. Symb. Logic, 1946) [1]. We place it within the modern framework of quantified modal logic with variable domains. We prove the completeness theorem using a property of a kind of normal form, and the fact that in the canonical frame, the relation on all maximal consistent sets, _R_ = _{⟨_ Γ _,_ ∆ _⟩_ : _∀A_ (✷ **_⊢_** _A ∈_ Γ = _⇒ A ∈_ ∆) _}_ , is a universal relation (both are unprovable in known quantified modal logics). The strength of the ✷ **_⊢_** operator is a proper extension of modal logic S5. Using completeness, we prove that satisfiability in a model of ✷ **_⊢_** _A_ under arbitrary valuation implies that _A_ is a thesis of formulated logic. So we can syntactically define logical entailment and consistency. Our semantics differ from S. Kripke’s standard one [3] in syntax, semantics, and interpretation of the necessity operator. We also have free variables, contrary to Kripke’s and Carnap’s approaches, but our notion of substitution is non-standard (variables inside modalities are not free). All ✷ **_⊢_** -free first-order theses are provable, as well as the Barcan formula and its converse. Our specific theses are ✷ **_⊢_** _A →∀xA_ , _¬_ ✷ **_⊢_** ( _x_ = _y_ ), _¬_ ✷ **_⊢_** _¬_ ( _x_ = _y_ ), _¬_ ✷ **_⊢_** _P_ ( _x_ ), _¬_ ✷ **_⊢_** _¬P_ ( _x_ ). We also have ✸ **_⊢_** _∃xA_ ( _x_ ) _→_ ✸ **_⊢_** _A_ (<sup>_y_</sup> _/x_ ), and _∀x_ ✷ **_⊢_** _A_ ( _x_ ) _→_ ✷ **_⊢_** _A_ (<sup>_y_</sup> _/x_ ), if _A_ is a ✷ **_⊢_** -free formula. **Key words** : R. Carnap, being a thesis operator; proper extension of S5 

## **1 Introduction** 

In 1946, R. Carnap formulated the _modal functional calculus_ (MFC), which is a version of quantified modal logic with an operator intended to express _logical necessity_ [1]. Carnap’s logic is not a typical modal quantified logic. It does not contain free variables, is stronger than S5 (formulas like _¬_ ✷ _∃xFx_ are provable), and identity holds only between the same variables or constants, although identical objects are necessarily identical. In short, MFC is far from contemporary first-order modal logic. Its models were built from sets of atomic formulas and their negations. There is no valuation and interpretation of predicates. Differences and similarities to Kripke’s semantics can be found in [4]. Recently, two important results on Carnap’s logic have been published. M. Cresswell proved 

1 

the completeness of a variant of Carnap’s axiomatization, keeping Carnap’s original semantics almost untouched [2]. M. Madows formulated modern non-modal semantics for Carnap’s logic, together with a corresponding tableau system [5]. Madows interpreted logical necessity in a first-order structures as a quantification over all (parametrically definable) subsets of the domain of the structure. We believe that his approach can be considered a faithful modern representation of Carnap’s approach to logical necessity. Carnap’s necessity, as well as the necessities from the two cited works, actually does not express being a first-order thesis. For example, formulas like ✷ _A →∀xA_ , _¬_ ✷ _∃xy_ ( _x_ = _y_ ), and _¬_ ✷ _∃xy_ ( _x_ = _y_ ) should be provable if we were to think of ✷ as a logical necessity. As far as we know, there is no formalized version of Carnap’s ideas in the literature where logical necessity explicitly expresses being a first-order thesis. We aim to build a first-order modal logic with an operator expressing first-order theses and interpret it in a variant of variable domain semantics. For simplicity, we do not introduce function symbols and constants. To show that our modality ✷ **_⊢_** is factually correct, we prove that the satisfiability of any formula ✷ **_⊢_** _A_ in any model implies its logical validity and being a thesis, as we prove the completeness theorem.

## **3 Formal system for being a thesis operator** 

It is a well-known fact that every proper extension of modal logic S5 is either not closed under the substitution rule or is closed under substitution but is characterized by a single frame with a finite domain, i.e., a frame with a fixed number of possible worlds [6]. Given that our models are infinite (for worlds and objects), we will formulate slightly different notions of free variable and substitution than the standard one. Our new notions and their counterparts from first-order logic are equivalent for formulas without ✷ **_⊢_** , but they differ for formulas with ✷ **_⊢_** to the one known from quantified modal logic.

In the end, we show that the satisfability of ✷ **_⊢_** _A_ in any model implies that _A ∈_ FOL✷ **_⊢_** . The final theorem demonstrates that identifying the modality ✷ **_⊢_** with the operator of being a thesis (and only a thesis) is substantively correct. **Theorem 4.** _If there is a model on universal structure A ∈_ UC _such that A, w, v |_ = ✷ **_⊢_** _A then A ∈_ FOL✷ **_⊢_** _, for any formula A._
